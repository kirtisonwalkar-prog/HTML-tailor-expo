Good to know
before you go.

Quick answers to help you plan your visit.

When is Tailors Expo?

The expo is scheduled for December 13 to 16, from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM each day.

Where is the venue?

Raiker Exhibition Ground, Hubli, Karnataka.

Who can visit?

Tailors, fashion designers, garment manufacturers, embroidery professionals, sewing machine dealers, students and general visitors are welcome.

Can I enquire about exhibiting?

Choose “Exhibiting / stall enquiry” in the interest form. The form is a front-end demo; connect it to your event team's email or registration system to receive submissions.
